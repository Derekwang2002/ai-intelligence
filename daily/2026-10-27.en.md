## Research

- **LIFT-Bench (new / TRIAL, late discovery):** A scoped constraint applies only to selected sections of a long document. The hard part is making the judge respect that scope instead of grading the whole answer uniformly. The public set contains 784 test tasks and 34,258 training tasks.
- **InteractiveWorld (new / WATCH, late discovery):** World-state verification checks whether the simulator actually changed rather than accepting the agent's narrative of success. The rubric still requires human adjudication.

## Developer Tools
