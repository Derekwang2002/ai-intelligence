## Models

### Kintsugi-27B (TRIAL)

Kintsugi compresses Qwen3.8-27B to 2.47 bits per weight and uses a custom runtime to fit full 262k context on a 12 GB GPU. Its trellis kernels decode multiple low-bit weights from packed codes. Stock llama.cpp, Ollama and LM Studio cannot load it, so model quality and operational cost need testing together.

### Inkling 2 Mini (TRIAL)

Two independent tests define complementary limits. It runs on local hardware and can perform well on data analysis, yet office, coding and long-loop cost are unstable. Scores from different harnesses are not directly comparable; rerun the actual workload with its real stop policy.
