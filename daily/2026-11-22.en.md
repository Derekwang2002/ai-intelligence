One leaderboard still cannot replace task-specific quality, latency and cost tests. [Source](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gemini-4-hydrogen)

## Models

### Inkling 2 Mini — TRIAL

Thinking Machines Lab released a multimodal MoE with about 26B total and 3B active parameters per token. A looped transformer executes the same middle layers repeatedly to reduce stored weights; it saves memory, but does not mean the model performs only one pass. Test time to first token, peak memory, long-context degradation and image-task quality on a 32GB machine.

### Gemini 4 Hydrogen — TRIAL (updated)

The independent MKA run reports a lower score than Google while confirming high output speed. This improves the evidence mix without changing the TRIAL recommendation: test quality, latency and total cost on the same real tasks.

## Agent & AI Engineering

Adversarial distillation means repeatedly querying a strong model and using its outputs to train or reinforce another model. The hard part is that individual requests can look benign; campaign-level risk may appear only across accounts and time. OpenAI’s internal result supports layered defenses, not a vendor-to-vendor comparison.
