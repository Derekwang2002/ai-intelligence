## Models

### GLM-5.4 (WATCH)

GLM-5.4 executes 28 shared middle layers twice to add computational depth with the same weights. A looped architecture sends intermediate representations through the same layers again for refinement; inference cost, cache design and marathon-thinking loops are the hard parts. Every Coding Plan tier, ZCode and Z.ai Chat can use it, but the standard API and weights are unavailable.

Sources: https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.4; https://zcode.z.ai/cn/docs/qa

### WeMM-Ecom (TRIAL)

Tencent uploaded 0.8B, 2B, 4B, 9B and 35B-A3B weights with GGUF, GPTQ and local serving instructions. The family targets product OCR, attribute extraction, moderation and multimodal understanding. Publisher aggregates across 92 benchmarks await independent reproduction.

Sources: https://huggingface.co/tencent/WeMM-Ecom-35B-A3B; https://huggingface.co/collections/tencent/wemm-ecom

### GPT-Live-1.1 (TRIAL)

OpenAI made GPT-Live-1.1 available on November 19. Active sessions cost $0.05 per minute and WebRTC standby while inference is paused costs $0.02. Multilingual transcription and accent gains are vendor claims; the date-only release is included as a targeted late discovery.

Sources: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog; https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-live-1.1

### LingSante-3.1-flash (WATCH)

InclusionAI published MIT weights for medical reasoning, evidence research and long-horizon tasks. A medical agent chains retrieval, judgment and tool calls into a workflow; an early error can compound through later steps. The official recipe needs eight H200s or four GB300s, and publisher benchmarks and simulations do not establish clinical effectiveness.

Source: https://huggingface.co/inclusionAI/LingSante-3.1-flash
