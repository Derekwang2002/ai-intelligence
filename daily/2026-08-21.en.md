## Agent & AI Engineering

- **Engineering implications of agentic retrieval** (see the Mistral event in the Executive Summary): once retrieval becomes model-driven multi-step document operation, the RAG management focus shifts from chunking strategy to tool-surface design and index freshness; document-dense domains (filings, contracts, compliance) benefit most, while simple direct lookups remain fine with one-shot RAG. The isolation-boundary design (index inside your perimeter) is the key enterprise-data selling point.
- **Hidden quantization × agent-memory interaction** (see Research): agent workspaces rely heavily on long, updatable, semantically dense contexts (memory systems, editable documents, tool-state) — exactly where INT4 proactive-interference losses are largest. Teams serving quantized open-weight models to agents should add overwrite-recall probes to acceptance testing.
- **Cost-exhaustion defenses for reasoning-model APIs** (see SMTrap in Research): attackers can synthesize inference-heavy queries without ever probing the target — weakening the assumptions behind rate limiting and query-pattern detection. Cheapest defenses: per-request token/cost ceilings plus solver-class tools so the model offloads instead of searching in-context.
- Orchestration implications of Codex `codex queue` (see Developer Tools): cross-session messaging + idle wake-up makes the orchestrator → multiple resident-sessions pattern usable in a stable CLI — another ingredient for the community orchestration-on-CLI pattern (trend #3 criterion (b)).
