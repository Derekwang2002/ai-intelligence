# AI Intelligence - 2026-11-15

## Daily Executive Summary

1. **Claude Code 2.1.325 closes several permission and sandbox gaps.** The stable release fixes compound Bash commands that could skip approval, allow rules that could launch another program, deny rules that failed after restart, and worktree and public-artifact boundaries. Sandboxed Bash now follows the normal permission flow by default, clarifying safety while potentially pausing unattended jobs for approval.
2. **Metal Sandbox publishes a native Apple Silicon execution path for coding agents.** It restricts file access with Apple App Sandbox and schedules Metal commands across Macs. The paper and code are public; experiments use four M4 Mac minis, and part of the throughput result comes from fixed-trace replay rather than end-to-end reruns.
3. **United Airlines starts blocking unauthorized personal AI agents from booking travel.** The restriction covers search, purchase, changes and trip management, while human-operated browsers and authorized industry systems are excepted. The announcement gives only a November 11 date and no public authorization interface, showing that transactional-agent deployment also needs service-side terms and authorization gates.
4. **Haru-4B makes full-duplex voice agents locally testable in a 4B model.** Apache-2.0 weights plus PyTorch, MLX, macOS and vLLM-Omni implementations are public, supporting simultaneous listening and speaking in nine languages. It remains a research base model without independently checkable conversation-quality, safety or real-interruption evaluation.

## Agent & AI Engineering

### Metal Sandbox

Trace replay applies a new scheduler to an already recorded sequence of agent commands instead of running the agent again. It is useful for comparing schedulers, but different feedback could change later agent behavior, so the 2.14x sustained-throughput figure is an estimate rather than an end-to-end reproduction.

Metal Sandbox supports Claude Code, Codex and mini-SWE-agent through Harbor. The paper explicitly says it is not hostile multi-tenant containment: some application-launch paths can remain outside the boundary, and other processes may still contend for the GPU.

### Haru-4B

Full duplex means both sides can speak and listen at the same time instead of waiting for turns. The hard part is handling an interruption while understanding new audio, stopping or revising outgoing speech and preserving conversation state. Haru-4B processes one 133 ms frame at a time and predicts audio plus text together, but no independent interruption-quality evaluation was found.

## Open Source

The Metal Sandbox implementation and Harbor fork are public and directly testable on Apple Silicon. There is no stable release or independent reproduction yet, making this a bounded TRIAL rather than a capacity-planning baseline.

Haru-4B publishes both weights and code, including PyTorch, MLX, a native macOS app and a vLLM-Omni plugin. The publisher recommends about 12 GB of GPU memory for the NVIDIA path; voice, latency and safety results remain independently unverified.

## Business & Policy

### United Airlines restricts personal AI agents

United says it will restrict unauthorized third-party personal AI agents from searching, booking, purchasing and managing travel through its channels or intermediaries. Bookings made before November 11 remain valid; future unauthorized-agent bookings can be canceled. The key boundary is not whether a model can operate a website, but whether the service permits it to take transactional consequences for a user.

The official page provides a publication date but no time. This item enters through recall review as a `late_discovery`, without claiming hour-level boundary verification.
