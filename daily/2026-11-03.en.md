## Open Source

### Numbat fake weights

`burner22/numbat` claims to contain a 7.72 TB checkpoint from an unreleased Anthropic model, without Anthropic authentication. A community audit regenerated 43 complete tensors bit-for-bit from pseudorandom seeds derived from tensor names, and an independent check reproduced nine samples. “Seeded noise” means the values follow a fixed random recipe rather than training; the hard part is that file size and internal consistency do not establish provenance. The repository is rated IGNORE and recorded as an overlap late discovery.

### vLLM 0.33

The stable release makes CUDA 13.0 the only CUDA release baseline, moves the ROCm default to 10.0 while retaining a 7.2 path, and adds an AWS EFA image, `/v1/decisions`, watchdogs and more low-precision KV paths. Speculative decoding drafts several tokens through a smaller or cheaper path and asks the main model to verify them; the hard part is that speedup depends on acceptance rate and workload, so project benchmarks do not transfer directly to production. The release notes still contain an artifact-link verification TODO, so teams should validate wheels, images, endpoint exposure and rollback.

## Infrastructure

### OpenAI Grid Protectors

OpenAI says future compute campuses will reduce load during grid stress and may supply power from on-site generation or storage. Demand response means a grid operator can ask a large customer to temporarily use less power; the hard questions are notice time, duration, workload impact and emissions. Georgia's 25-year agreement is approved, with about 3.2 GW of planned demand and up to 1 GW of flexible response, but the campus is still in development. Georgia Power's $950 million annual figure is a portfolio projection across large-load agreements, not OpenAI-only or realized savings.

### NVIDIA Workload Map

Workload Map uses a CRD and Go library to turn JobSet, PyTorchJob, Ray, KServe and other Kubernetes objects into a common tree. A CRD adds a new resource type to Kubernetes; the difficult part is reconciling how frameworks express replicas, resources and status. NVIDIA reports reuse by Run:ai, KAI Scheduler and Elotl Nova, but independent production validation is still missing.

## Business & Policy

### Anthropic S-1/A

Anthropic filed Amendment No. 1 to its S-1 with unaudited interim results through September 30: $17.0 billion of third-quarter revenue and approximately $895.4 billion of compute and infrastructure commitments, including about $447.5 billion expected before 2031. Contractual commitments are not cash already paid. The nine-month net loss also includes $94.3 billion of fair-value changes in convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs, so it is not a direct operating-loss measure. The filing gives a November 2 date but no publication time, so this brief does not claim hour-level boundary verification.

Grid Protectors is an infrastructure commitment, not observed operating performance. xAI's September-announced redirect for `grok-imagine-image-quality` became effective in this window with an unchanged request shape and lower price, so it was filtered as a planned lifecycle transition.
