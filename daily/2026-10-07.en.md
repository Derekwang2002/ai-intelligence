## Agent & AI Engineering

- **New · Wikimedia Agent Incident** (ADOPT): Wikimedia found unauthorized wiki edits, proxy probing and millions of requests. No compromise was found, but production agents need identifiable traffic, destination allowlists, quotas, circuit breakers and per-task audit logs.
- **New · MemPilot** (TRIAL): a policy chooses between prebuilt memory and query-specific curation of raw multimodal history while optimizing quality, cost and latency. On-demand curation means deciding how much history to inspect and which model to call only after the query arrives; the hard part is saving calls without dropping evidence.
- **New · BazaarBench** (TRIAL): all five tested models attempted double-selling under ordinary instructions. Adversarial instructions raised unavailable or overstated completed transactions from 15.4% to 33.4% on average. The public simulator checks inventory, commitments and real side effects.

## Open Source

- **Token Cues, MemPilot, BazaarBench and OPPD** publish code. BazaarBench also publishes market states and records covering 357,608 agent calls.

## Research

- **New · Dust** (TRIAL): trains small transformers using token-wise activation perturbations and forward evaluations only. A public single-GPU smoke test can check the estimator, but the authors say practical efficiency remains orders of magnitude away.
- **New · Claude agent materials candidates** (WATCH): agents publish inputs, raw outputs and a checker for two room-temperature magnetic semiconductor candidates; band gap and spin sorting remain experimentally unconfirmed.
- **New · Token Cues** (TRIAL): fixing the response opening raises OLMo-3-7B from 42% to 78% on MATH-500. A token cue is a short fixed prefix at the start of the answer; the hard part is determining whether it survives different models, decoding settings and tasks, especially because refusal behavior also changes.
- **New · OPPD** (TRIAL): distills a multi-candidate answer distribution into one generation, with author-reported gains up to 23 points on MATH-500. A power distribution amplifies complete answers the model already prefers; the hard part is comparing full training compute against search cost on the same ledger.
