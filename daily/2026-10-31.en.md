## Models

### Clover-35B-A3B-Preview

A diffusion language model revises groups of tokens in parallel instead of extending text one token at a time like a common autoregressive model. Its decoding, caching and throughput metrics therefore need different validation. The public preview makes the approach testable, but publisher benchmarks remain unreproduced.

### Lumma-Fev-v2

Calibration asks whether model confidence matches real success rates. A model can be accurate yet dangerously overconfident when wrong. The public 4B/27B weights can now be checked on an internal decision set.
