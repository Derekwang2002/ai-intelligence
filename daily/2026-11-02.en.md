### Auctopus

The paper reports detecting drift in one quantum-spin experiment, rejecting an inconclusive result and revising the protocol. Drift means experimental conditions move over time; the hard part is distinguishing that movement from noise. Public code omits the prompts and instrument description for the real study, so this remains WATCH.

## Business & Policy

### Shared AI-training preference signal

An individual Internet-Draft defines the __usage_ai-training token in robots.txt. It expresses a training preference, not access control: crawlers can still access content, and it creates neither retroactive deletion nor enforcement. OpenAI, Google and Microsoft have different support timelines, so current crawler rules should remain.

### NVIDIA × Hugging Face

The SEC filing confirms $11.9 billion in cash plus up to $1 billion in retention consideration, with closing expected in the first half of 2027 subject to approvals. Reuters, citing two sources, reports DOJ competition inquiries. An inquiry is not a finding of wrongdoing, and no public DOJ document exists.
