### 2. OpenAI ships healthcare computer-use access and CVS publishes CUA Agent Kit — new event

- OpenAI announced on 2026-11-16 that computer-use agents are available today through the OpenAI API, ChatGPT for Healthcare, and ChatGPT for Clinicians. ChatGPT for Healthcare also adds improved clinical search and more than 20 healthcare plugins; access depends on workspace settings, eligible U.S. clinician verification, or sales-led organization access. HIPAA-supporting use requires eligible services under a BAA plus the required security and retention settings. The pages provide a date but no publication time. [OpenAI computer-use report](https://openai.com/index/advancing-computer-use-for-healthcare-teams/) · [ChatGPT for Healthcare update](https://openai.com/index/more-ways-to-move-care-forward-with-chatgpt-for-healthcare/)
- OpenAI reports two distinct evaluation sets: 99% of 325 reviewed clinical tasks rated safe, and 93% of 646 physician assessments rated very/extremely helpful. These are not diagnosis accuracy rates. On HealthAdminBench, OpenAI reports GPT-6 Astra at 94.3% of rubric points and GPT-6 Luna at 91.8%, with Luna costing 8.5 cents/task at maximum reasoning effort. The public benchmark has 135 synthetic tasks and 1,698 verifiers; rubric-point scores must not be compared directly with the original paper's strict end-to-end task-success rate (best reported there was 36.3%). [Stanford HealthAdminBench](https://aims.stanford.edu/measurement-db/healthadminbench) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09937)
- The operational evidence is stronger than a demo but still comes through OpenAI and named partners: CVS says one production agent cleared a months-long document backlog over a weekend and another handles system steps for tens of thousands of consults per day, with validation, human oversight, screenshots, and action records. Cigna says it moved from concept to deployment in under eight weeks. No independent audit or error-rate distribution was published.
- CVS's Apache-2.0 [CUA Agent Kit](https://github.com/cvs-health/cua-agent-kit) is public. Its practical pattern is important: an OpenAI computer-use agent discovers a workflow and records evidence, then Codex converts successful runs into deterministic Playwright or pywinauto code that runs without an LLM. The repository was created 2026-10-30 and last code-updated 2026-11-12, so 2026-11-16 is the announcement/public-discovery date, not the repository's creation date. The README explicitly warns that screenshots, traces and logs may contain credentials, cookies, tokens and visible sensitive data; redaction is best-effort.
- Recommendation boundary: `TRIAL` is defensible for the open kit as a one-day synthetic-workflow evaluation; healthcare production claims should remain `WATCH` unless local security, BAA scope, rollback, approval, and audit requirements are validated.
