- Technical details:
  - DeepSelect becomes a selectable sparse-indexer TopK backend for DeepSeek Sparse Attention. On GB200 at batch 256 / 1M KV, the PR reports 191 µs versus 616 µs for the previous path (~3.2× faster for this kernel); bf16 is 119 µs versus 529 µs for FlashInfer. Scope is Blackwell and this kernel, not end-to-end serving.

A two-node TP1×DP8 run validates both modes; shared tables eliminate per-step Engram DP communication. Prefetch improves measured TTFT by up to 0.9% with no decode regression in the reported 4×GB200 tests.
- Engineering judgment: **update `ev-20260910-01` (DeepSeek V4.1-Flash); keep TRIAL**. The model's open-weight serving story advances from a burst of issues/PRs to merged framework paths with benchmark and cross-node validation. The LoRA merge can also be cross-linked to the existing DeepSeek V4 Flash Vision event, but its PR has no test results and is not independently material.
