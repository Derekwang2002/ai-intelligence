### 11. ASLEval finds endpoint-only agent privacy checks miss almost half of exposure

- Primary source: [arXiv 2609.18864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18864), submitted `2026-09-16T16:03:11Z`.
- Finding: ASLEval pre-registers hidden target data, models authorization, and evaluates every declared visible exit in a multi-step session rather than only the expected action, final response or attacker report. Across multiple enterprise-style environments and independently implemented runtimes, the expected-outlet-only view missed 46.9% of exposure recovered by the full visible-exit union.
- Engineering implication: privacy evaluation needs a complete, explicit egress boundary and must report task utility alongside leakage; simply reducing model-visible returns can suppress normal task success.
- Caveat: no public code repository is linked and the abstract does not enumerate model/runtime names. Treat the result as a benchmark design signal pending artifact review.
- Dedup: no matching title, arXiv ID or framework exists in the index. Treat as a new agent-privacy research event.

### 14. Simple internal vectors detect and anticipate reward hacking
