### H Company releases Holo4 for GUI, code, MCP and API work

- Classification: **new event**, `ai-agent` / `open-source`; suggested `TRIAL`.
- Event date: `2026-09-28`. The official newsroom page carries `article:published_time=2026-09-28T00:00:00.000Z`; the Hugging Face feed published the companion post at `2026-09-28T09:44:05Z`. The public model repositories were updated with their release material at roughly `2026-09-28T14:34Z`, before this run started.
- Suggested fingerprint: `hcompany-holo4-generalist-computer-use-open-weights`.
- Suggested title: `H Company releases Holo4 to work across screens, code and APIs`.
- Suggested Chinese title: `H Company 发布跨屏幕、代码和 API 的 Holo4`.

What happened:

- H Company released two Holo4 vision-language agents: a dense 27B model based on Qwen3.8-27B and a 35B-A3B MoE based on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B. Both expose a 262,144-token configured context window.
- The same model/harness can click and type in desktop, web and Android interfaces, execute code, and call MCP or conventional API tools. H Company says its task factory produced about 10,000 tasks spanning web apps, desktop environments and MCP servers; post-training used supervised learning plus asynchronous online reinforcement learning.
- Availability is concrete: both models are live on H Models API; ungated BF16/FP8/NVFP4/GGUF weights are linked from the Hugging Face collection; the `hai-agents` harness is public; and H Company published the evaluation trajectories for public benchmarks.
- License is split and must not be flattened into “open source”: `Holo4-27B` is CC BY-NC 4.0 and therefore non-commercial, while `Holo4-35B-A3B` is Apache 2.0. Holotron4 Nano is a related NVIDIA-base release under the NVIDIA Open Model Agreement.

Vendor-reported results and limits:

- Holo4-27B reports 85.2% on OSWorld at a claimed $0.08/task and 61.7% average partial score / 41.5% success on OSWorld 2.0 at $1.22/task. Holo4-35B-A3B reports 80.8% on OSWorld and 30.9% / 12.3% on OSWorld 2.0.
- The newsroom explicitly says OSWorld 2.0 and ALE-CLI are single runs. Comparisons to frontier models mix harnesses, task subsets and effort levels, so the cross-model table is not an apples-to-apples independent evaluation.
- AutomationBench public-set scores are 45.4% for 27B and 34.5% for 35B-A3B. Of its 600 public tasks, 480 fall in the split used to collect training data. On the 120 held-out tasks, H Company reports 49.3% and 31.7%, respectively. The models have not yet been evaluated on AutomationBench's official private set.
- The published trajectories materially reduce verification cost, but they are evidence for reproducing the vendor run, not independent validation.

Why it matters:

For teams building computer-use agents, Holo4 is an unusually testable release: weights, harness and step-by-step trajectories are available together, and one model can switch between pixels, shell/code and structured tools. Trial the Apache-2.0 35B-A3B model first for commercial evaluation, despite its weaker vendor-reported long-workflow score. Treat the 27B model as research-only unless a separate commercial license is obtained. Re-run a small OSWorld 2.0 and held-out AutomationBench slice in the team's own harness before accepting the cost/performance claims.
