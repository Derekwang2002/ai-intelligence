### 1. OpenAI launches opt-in text watermarking with textGrain

**Recommended disposition:** `new-event` (WATCH).

OpenAI published the policy and technical report at `2026-10-05T15:00:00Z`. API customers can opt in globally for selected models starting October 5; eligible ChatGPT and Codex text in the EU will gain invisible watermarks over the following weeks. Detector access is initially restricted to approved researchers and expert organizations. The detector is therefore not a generally available integration surface yet.

The public technical report describes an entropy-calibrated, keyed optimal-transport watermark. OpenAI reports roughly 80% detection on 200-token psychology passages and 95% on 400-token passages at a 1% target false-positive rate. Editing is a hard boundary: synonym replacement of 10% of words reduced reported detection from about 92% to 66%, and replacing 25% reduced it to 17%. OpenAI says it plans to open-source the technology, but no public implementation was linked at publication time.
