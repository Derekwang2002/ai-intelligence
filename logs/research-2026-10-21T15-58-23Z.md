### 3. FairStress measures demographic sensitivity under adversarial framing

- Primary source: [Allen Institute for AI / Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/fairstress)
- Publication: `2026-10-20T16:09:19Z` in the HF feed; exact timestamp and inside the main interval.
- Stage and access: benchmark, data and code are public. The full benchmark expands 300 two-candidate scenarios across 29 domains to more than 13 million test cases; FairStress-Core is a 50,000-case subset.
- Evidence: 15 models were run on the full benchmark. The authors report little difference without demographic context, larger asymmetry when identity is explicit, and average correct-answer abandonment gaps of 6.9 percentage points for implied identity and 9.3 points for explicit identity. Activation analysis covered only two models and a small set of contrasts. Nine tested mitigations mostly failed; counterfactual chain-of-thought reduced but did not remove the gap.
- Limits: the benchmark operationalizes fairness as equality under matched demographic swaps; it does not settle every fairness definition or real-world harm direction. Results must not be rewritten as a universal claim that every model discriminates in one direction.
- Deduplication: no match found; `new-event`.
- Editorial value: public materials make a one-day model/guardrail evaluation practical; `TRIAL` is supportable with the stated construct-validity limits.
