### AI21 LIFT-Bench / LIFT-Train — public scoped instruction-following benchmark

- AI21's blog metadata gives `datePublished=2026-10-25T08:52:27Z`, before the overlap; its `dateModified=2026-10-26T13:02:26Z` is in-window but the page exposes no decision-changing change log. The paper was submitted October 20. The repo was created October 18 and last pushed October 25 at `11:33:18Z`, also before overlap.
- Public artifacts are unusually complete: Apache-2.0 code and synthesis pipeline, 784 test tasks over 10 splits, and 34,258 training tasks. Constraints are scoped to document sections and mix deterministic checks with an LLM judge.
- AI21 reports that an IFTrain run reached perfect reward while median output length collapsed from 114 to 18 words; scoped judging improved macro-F1 by 11–19 points on 305 human-labelled examples. These are first-party results, not independent reproduction. The authors describe outside-benchmark deltas as small and as avoiding regression, not a general win.
- Reproduction caveat: scores are comparable only with the same judge endpoint; the released judge name is `google/gemma-4-31B-it`. Editorial read: potentially `TRIAL` as a late discovery for teams training/evaluating long structured outputs, with a one-day local smoke test on a pinned split and judge.
