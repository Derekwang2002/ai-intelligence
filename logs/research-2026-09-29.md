### 2. MCP TypeScript SDK 2.2 tightens OAuth and changes list-call behavior — KEEP, TRIAL

### 5. Failure-Transparent Agents turns post-tool-failure evidence into an enforceable contract — KEEP, TRIAL/WATCH

- arXiv v1 published `2026-09-28T17:51:41Z`.
- Across 100 deterministic failure tasks, six models, and 3,600 human-annotated responses, the authors report false-success falling from 22.8% at baseline to 9.3% with transparency instructions and 0.8% with a structured evidence contract; useful responses rise from 74.9% to 98.8%.
- The artifact is actionable for agent reliability work: require evidence for claimed completion after a blocked or failed tool call. The benchmark is controlled rather than a full end-to-end deployment, so retain the result as frontier evidence pending independent reproduction.
- Sources: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35732), [MIT-licensed code and data](https://github.com/junru-zhu/failure-transparent-agents).
- Deduplication: no title or concept match in the existing event store.
