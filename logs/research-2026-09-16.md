## Retain as new candidates

### 1. OPEN-1B makes every training step independently replayable

- Primary source: [arXiv 2609.17380](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17380), submitted `2026-09-15T16:23:08Z`.
- Availability: [Apache-2.0 training and audit code](https://github.com/gensyn-ai/open-transformers) plus an ungated [OPEN-1B Hugging Face collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/Gensyn/open-1b). The base, 93B-token midtrained, and SFT checkpoints are public; their repositories report Apache-2.0 metadata and roughly 1.61B safetensors parameters each.
- Why it qualifies: the authors impose deterministic ordering on kernel reductions, batch ordering, and distributed collectives so an auditor can replay any training step on commodity hardware with bitwise certainty. They release the dataset, every intermediate checkpoint, training code, and audit harness. This is a concrete open-model provenance mechanism, not a transparency pledge.
- Caveat: the 1B scale is a systems proof point; cost and practicality at frontier scale remain unproven.
- Dedup: no arXiv ID, title, URL, OPEN-1B product, or matching fingerprint exists in the 157-event index. Treat as a new research/open-source event.

### 2. FlashVector optimizes an entire production serving stack with an agent

- Primary source: [arXiv 2609.17391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17391), submitted `2026-09-15T16:27:45Z`.
- Availability: paper and source are public; no implementation repository is linked. The system is reported as deployed on Unity's Vector advertising platform.
- Why it qualifies: FlashVector extends agentic optimization beyond isolated GPU kernels into framework graphs, NVIDIA Triton server code, and Python feature transformation. The authors report up to 2x model-server throughput, 1.98x latency improvement, and 1.6x feature-store throughput after production deployment.
- Caveat: results are author-reported on one production platform and cannot yet be independently reproduced.
- Dedup: no arXiv ID, title, URL, or FlashVector entity exists in the index. It is adjacent to kernel-agent and serving-efficiency records but is not the same event.

### 3. SWE-bench's top ranks are statistically unresolved

- Primary source: [arXiv 2609.17394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17394), submitted `2026-09-15T16:29:12Z`; accepted at ADMA 2026.
- Reproduction package: [Adkid-Zephyr/resolution-audit](https://github.com/Adkid-Zephyr/resolution-audit).
- Why it qualifies: the audit covers 254 submissions across four SWE-bench splits. None of the 29 adjacent pairs in the Verified top 30 separates at alpha 0.05 under exact paired McNemar tests, and observed within-model scaffold ranges reach 29.8 points versus an 8.8-point top-30 spread. The released protocol replaces tiny aggregate-score differences with paired resolution and model-scaffold provenance.
- Caveat: it analyzes published verdicts rather than rerunning models; the paper explicitly avoids causal claims about scaffold effects.
- Dedup: distinct from the indexed RealSWE, SWE-Gate, SWE-Bench Pro, and exploit-audit events. Those question realism or reward integrity; this event tests whether leaderboard rank gaps are statistically resolvable.

### 4. Decomposition reduces root-context exposure but loses findings at every tier

- Primary source: [arXiv 2609.17464](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17464), submitted `2026-09-15T17:04:09Z`.
- Availability: paper source includes an extensive ancillary code bundle and analysis scripts directly on arXiv.
- Why it qualifies: the paper models and measures information loss across multi-agent delegation trees using 600 production deep-research traces, 1,012 annotated multi-agent traces, and 743,819 production tool calls. It estimates a per-tier yield penalty, while showing that deeper decomposition reduces persistent root-context exposure and can win on cost only beyond a workload threshold.
- Caveat: one author and one undisclosed production system; the underlying production traces are not presented as an independently reusable dataset.
- Dedup: no exact or product-level collision. It is a new empirical constraint on the already-established multi-agent-runtime trend, not a new trend by itself.

### 5. JustFit demonstrates 200K-token local serving on a 24 GiB laptop

- Primary source: [arXiv 2609.17475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17475), submitted `2026-09-15T17:15:48Z`.
- Availability: the paper links the tested [MLX-VLM implementation branch and exact commits](https://github.com/YuhuaBillChen/mlx-vlm/tree/production/qwen-paged-continuous-batching); the experiment uses Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4 on an M4 Pro MacBook.
- Why it qualifies: compressed KV execution, just-in-time component residency, and state-preserving serving transitions complete 196,608 input plus 16,384 output tokens in three runs, 6.93x the baseline's completed single-request context. It reports 19.11 tok/s on a separate 32K-input probe and 29/30 AIME 2026 answers.
- Caveat: single-author, single-machine, single-model evaluation; the performance and quality claims need third-party reproduction.
- Dedup: no arXiv ID/title/JustFit collision. It is complementary to the indexed local open-weight serving signal, not a duplicate of a model release.

### 6. ScienceBuddy releases a workspace that co-evolves its agent harness and model

- Primary source: [arXiv 2609.17523](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17523), submitted `2026-09-15T17:55:28Z`.
- Availability: [Gen-Verse/ScienceBuddy](https://github.com/Gen-Verse/ScienceBuddy) is public under MIT and includes the workspace, co-evolution pipeline, training script, configs, experiment documentation, and CI.

It is a usable research product with code, not only a proposed architecture.
- Caveat: the abstract reports case studies across four task families rather than broad independent benchmark results; maturity is research-demo level.
- Dedup: no arXiv ID, title, repository, or ScienceBuddy entity exists in the index. Treat as a new open-source research-agent event.

### 7. Social-harness experiments expose failures between otherwise competent agents

- Primary source: [arXiv 2609.17527](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17527), submitted `2026-09-15T17:57:27Z`.
- Evidence release: [social-harness/social-harness-paper](https://github.com/social-harness/social-harness-paper) publishes configs, inputs, transcripts, verdicts, and run results used by the paper.
- Why it qualifies: the work separates each agent's personal harness from a proposed cross-principal social harness. Its layered design targets prevention, runtime invalid-message detection, and post-incident investigation when honest, faulty, or malicious agents coordinate across trust boundaries.
- Caveat: this is an architecture and experimental evidence package, not a production implementation or protocol standard.
- Dedup: no exact collision. It is new evidence for the established multi-agent-runtime trend and the emerging capability/policy-separation trend, not sufficient to create another trend.

### 8. GitHub broadens AI Scan beyond repositories using CodeQL default setup
